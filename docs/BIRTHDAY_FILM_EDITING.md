# Birthday film editing

## Two ways to play

1. **Birthday Theatre (browser).** *5th Birthday → ▶ Play Swritsinh's 5th Birthday.* Fullscreen slideshow with the
   opening banner, chapter cards, your captions, gentle motion (none if "reduce motion" is on), and the closing
   banner. Works immediately, no render needed. Keys: Space play/pause, ←/→ scene, F fullscreen, M mute.
   Browsers need one tap before sound can play. TV browsers and casting vary.
2. **MP4 file (most reliable on a big TV).** *Make a video file for the TV → Render 1080p MP4*, then *Download MP4*
   and copy it to a USB stick. The worker renders it, checks it with ffprobe (resolution, 24 fps, H.264 + AAC
   stereo, duration) and samples frames for black screens before it is offered. 4K (3840×2160) is available but
   slow and needs a bigger worker.

Earlier films you upload (e.g. the existing 4K edits) appear under **Past films** and play directly.

## How the film is built

- Only photos with a **confirmed date** up to the birthday are used (undated ones wait in the review tray).
- Chapters follow his age: *The Beginning* (first year) → *Little Moments, Big Wonders* (1–2½) →
  *Family & Love* (photos you put in the **Family & Love** album) → *Growing Up* (2½–4) → *Our Little Explorer* (4–5).
- Photos in the album **Our First Five Years** are always included. Favourites are preferred and stay a little longer.
  Everything else is sampled evenly across the years to fit the target length (default 3 minutes).
- Captions on screen are **only what you wrote** (on the photo, or in the studio). The film never invents
  "first steps" or places.
- Portrait photos are shown whole over a blurred fill: never stretched, never cropped through faces.

## Studio

*Edit the film* lets you change the title, opening and closing lines, target length, per-photo captions,
**Pin** (always include) and **Leave out**. Editing returns the film to *draft*; *Approve* when happy, then render.
Nothing is published or shared automatically.

## Music

- Default: an **original instrumental** generated by the app, then the traditional **"Happy Birthday to You"
  melody as an instrumental** for the finale. It is labelled on screen as instrumental. No voices are generated.
- To use a real song (for example the family singing "Happy birthday, dear Swritsinh"), upload an MP3/M4A/WAV on the
  5th Birthday page and tick *This recording is ours / we have permission*. The finale starts early enough for the
  whole song to play. A recorded song is only mixed into a downloaded MP4 after that box is ticked.
- Do not upload commercial recordings you don't have rights to.

## Tips for tonight

1. Confirm his birth date in Settings.
2. Clear the *Dates to check* tray so those photos can join the film.
3. Add 15–30 must-have photos to *Our First Five Years*; mark a few favourites.
4. Write captions on the photos that need one.
5. Play the Theatre once end-to-end, then render the 1080p MP4 and copy it to a USB stick as backup.
